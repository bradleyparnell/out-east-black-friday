Three campaign directions for a brand that should never look like a doorbuster.

The Brief

“Every bottle should feel like
an invitation, not a flex.”

— Out East founders

The tension

Black Friday is loud, discounted, and urgent. Out East is unhurried, warm, and quietly assured.

The opportunity

Be the calm, beautiful pause in a crowded feed. Premium in every detail. Approachable at $39.99.

The rule

No red slashes. No countdown clocks. Lead with the feeling; let the price and the six-bottle shipping benefit do the persuading.
